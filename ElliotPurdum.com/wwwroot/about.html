About

Elliot Purdum is a cellist and conductor based in Berea, Ohio.

He is currently a student at Baldwin Wallace University, where he studies cello performance.

Elliot has continued his cello studies at the Sewanee Summer Music Festival, and has studied conducting with several mentors, including Markand Thakar, Erik Rohde, and Douglas Droste.

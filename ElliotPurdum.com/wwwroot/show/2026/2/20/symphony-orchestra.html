Friday, February 20, 2026 — 7:00 PM

Venue
Gamble Auditorium, Berea, OH
Program
Rózsa Concerto for Viola and Orchestra (Roy Marcos '27, viola — Lauria Concerto Competition winner); world premiere by Ilona Fiedorowicz '28; Rimsky-Korsakov Capriccio Espagnol
Role
section

The mid-season concert centers on Lauria Concerto Competition winner Roy Marcos in Miklós Rózsa's Viola Concerto — a 20th-century rarity that turns the viola's natural reticence into a strength. Bookending it: a world premiere by student composer Ilona Fiedorowicz, and the orchestral showpiece Capriccio Espagnol.

Fall Orchestral Concert

Saturday, September 12, 2026 — 7:30 PM

Venue
Gamble Auditorium, Berea, OH
Program
Brahms Symphony No. 4 in E minor; Barber Adagio for Strings
Role
conductor

The fall concert opens with Barber's Adagio for Strings — a work whose unhurried expansion of a single melodic line continues to define what string ensembles sound like at their most concentrated.

The second half is Brahms's Fourth, his last symphony and arguably his most unflinching: a passacaglia finale built on a baroque ground bass, emotional weight without sentimentality.

Symphony Orchestra and Pops — At the Movies

Thursday, May 1, 2025 — 7:00 PM

Venue
Gamble Auditorium, Berea, OH
Program
Music from Superman, Star Wars, The Wizard of Oz, Breaking Away, and a student film score by Hank Temple '26
Role
section

"BW Pops: At the Movies" closes the 2024–25 season with a film-score program — Superman, Star Wars, The Wizard of Oz, Breaking Away — alongside a student-produced short film and original soundtrack by composition major Hank Temple. The Symphony Orchestra shares the stage with members of the BW Music Theatre Class of 2025.

BW Symphony Orchestra

Friday, September 27, 2024 — 7:30 PM

Venue
Gamble Auditorium, Berea, OH
Program
Tchaikovsky Symphony No. 5 in E minor, Op. 64
Role
section

The 2024–25 season opener pairs the BW Symphony Orchestra with violin faculty Dr. Steve Sang Koh. The main work is Tchaikovsky's Fifth Symphony — a piece whose recurring "fate" motto threads through all four movements, transforming from somber march to triumphal hymn by the close.

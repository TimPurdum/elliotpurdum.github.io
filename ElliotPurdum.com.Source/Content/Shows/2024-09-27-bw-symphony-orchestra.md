The 2024–25 season opener pairs the BW Symphony Orchestra with violin
faculty Dr. Steve Sang Koh. The main work is Tchaikovsky's Fifth Symphony —
a piece whose recurring "fate" motto threads through all four movements,
transforming from somber march to triumphal hymn by the close.

The fall concert opens with Barber's Adagio for Strings — a work whose unhurried
expansion of a single melodic line continues to define what string ensembles
sound like at their most concentrated.

The second half is Brahms's Fourth, his last symphony and arguably his most
unflinching: a passacaglia finale built on a baroque ground bass, emotional
weight without sentimentality.

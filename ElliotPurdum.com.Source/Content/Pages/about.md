# About

Elliot Purdum is a cellist and conductor based in Berea, Ohio.

He is currently a student at [Baldwin Wallace University](https://www.bw.edu/),
where he studies cello performance.

Elliot has continued his cello studies at the
[Sewanee Summer Music Festival](https://www.sewaneemusicfestival.org/),
and has studied conducting with several mentors, including
Markand Thakar, Erik Rohde, and Douglas Droste.
